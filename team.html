Editor in Chief

Hello! My name is Marlow Ryberg and I am an aspiring premed student with a particular interest in forensic pathology. I love science, learning, art and the outdoors. When I’m not in school, I’m doing something in my community whether that’s volunteering at KidPower or the Academy of Children’s Theater or spending time with my friends! This year I’m the editor in chief and looking forward to improving my medical knowledge and editorial skills before college!
