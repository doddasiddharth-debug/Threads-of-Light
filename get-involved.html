Get involved

There is a job here for whatever you are good at

Threads of Light runs entirely on student volunteers. No experience required, no minimum hours, no fee, and every role counts toward community service hours.

Writers & editors

Interview storytellers, draft their pieces, and edit with care. You will get a style guide, a partner, and feedback on everything you write.

Design & social media

Turn each campaign into graphics, carousels, and short video. If you already run an account or open Canva for fun, this is your team.

Outreach

Connect with patient organizations, clinics, and schools to find storytellers and partners for upcoming months.

Research & fact-checking

Make sure every claim we publish traces back to a credible source. Ideal if you are interested in medicine or public health.

Chapters

Start Threads of Light at your school

A chapter is a small team at your school or university that runs the monthly campaign locally: assemblies, posters, a display case, an Instagram takeover, whatever fits your community.

We give you the campaign materials, a chapter handbook, design templates, and a monthly call with the core team. You bring three or more students and a staff advisor.

Ask about starting a chapter

What a chapter gets

  • Ready-to-print posters and slides for every campaign month
  • A handbook covering meetings, events, and outreach
  • Your chapter listed on this site
  • Support from the core team on a monthly call
  • Verified service hours for every member

Other ways to help

Not looking for a weekly commitment?

Share a campaign

Repost the month's stories, or print a poster for a noticeboard. Reach is the whole point of awareness work.

Introduce us

Know a patient organization, a clinic, or someone with a story worth telling? An introduction helps more than you would think.

Partner with us

Organizations and clubs can co-run a campaign month with us. Get in touch and we will figure out what that looks like.

Ready when you are

Tell us what you would like to do and we will put you on a team before the next campaign starts.
